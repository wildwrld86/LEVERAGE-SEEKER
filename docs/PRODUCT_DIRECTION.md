# LEVERAGE//SEEKER — research asset to useful product

**North star:** Give independent builders credible, timely answers to: *What changed? Am I eligible? What is it really worth? When does it expire? What should I do? What have similar opportunities actually delivered?*

## Phase A: Accumulation (current; no founder time needed)
- Collect verified material deltas plus closed/rejected opportunities, with dated sources.
- Keep a stable subject ID and immutable history across weeks.
- Rate source quality, eligibility friction, likely usefulness, and compounding patterns.
- Distinguish public evidence from private founder/customer outcomes.
- Skip days without meaningful findings; avoid bloated daily content.

## Phase B: Free utility (only after the feed is trustworthy)
- A searchable, mobile-friendly feed of current verified programs and critical changes.
- Dates, source links, cost/eligibility caveats, and visible rejection history.
- RSS/JSON export as practical; no misleading 'guaranteed eligibility' claims.
- Distribution through public proof, SEO, developer communities and user referrals; never spam.

## Phase C: Paid experiment (ONLY with evidence of user demand)
Possible paid features: saved eligibility profiles, relevant early change alerts, personalized watchlists, actionable application packs, research digests, and a source-backed API. Price and billing integration are **unvalidated**. Avoid subscription build and Stripe dependency until users demonstrate a valuable recurring use case.

## Why this might compound
The defensible asset isn't an ever-growing list of links. It's **time-stamped changes, expired opportunities, reliable eligibility checks, avoided false positives, and measured outcomes**. Measure coverage, recency, prediction accuracy and real utility over raw listing count.

## Safety and permissions
Public Git is for facts that are safe to publish. Paid user profiles, applications, identities, payment data, customer records and personal outcomes belong in protected systems, never this repo. No automatic third-party outreach, applications, spending or billing.

## Near-term anchor
The active service hypothesis is **SECONDS MATTER**: missed-call lead response and callback accountability for small businesses. Its buyer validation matters but it should not consume the research engine or force the owner to execute while unavailable.
